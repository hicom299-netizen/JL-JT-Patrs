WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Products that fit the vehicle — and partners who fit the business.

JL-JT-PARTS is focused on accessories and upgrade components for the Wrangler JL and Gladiator JT platform. We are interested in manufacturers who combine engineering capability, stable quality, competitive pricing and reliable communication.

PRODUCT FOCUS

Parts with real aftermarket potential.
